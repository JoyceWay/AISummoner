NIGHT 01 · 第一夜

INTERVIEW 01 · ROBERT LAWRENCE KUHN: CLOSER TO TRUTH罗伯特·劳伦斯·库恩：走近真相

Tonight answers just one question: what does quantum theory mean — and what is ultimate reality? The answer starts with a coin toss that splits the world.

今夜只回答一个问题：量子理论是什么意思——终极实在又是什么？答案从一次抛硬币开始，那次抛硬币分裂了世界。

SCENE 1 · OPENINGA coin toss splits the world一次抛硬币，分裂了世界

Robert Lawrence Kuhn opens with the difficulty itself. The many-worlds picture of quantum theory: innumerable different universes, continuously branching. It is, he says, very difficult to understand.

罗伯特·劳伦斯·库恩一上来就直说难处。量子理论的多世界图像：无数个不同的宇宙，不断分支。他说，这非常难理解。

Deutsch corrects the picture at once. The universes do not branch or split. They differentiate. At the beginning of time, they may all have been identical — and physical processes, following the laws of quantum mechanics, made them different from each other. The total number stays constant. What grows, very rapidly, is the degree of differentiation between them.

Deutsch 马上纠正了这个图像。宇宙不是分支、不是分裂，而是分化（differentiate）。在时间之初，它们可能全都一模一样——物理过程遵循量子力学定律，让它们彼此不同。总数不变，变的是它们之间的分化程度，而且分化得非常快。

And that growth is what we call the arrow of time. Time's one-way flow is not a separate ingredient of reality. It is what increasing differentiation looks like, from the inside.

而这种增长，就是我们说的时间之箭。时间单向流逝不是实在里另外一种配料，它是分化不断加深、从内部看起来的样子。

Take a coin toss. Once the coin lands, the version of you that saw heads and the version that saw tails no longer interact — their effect on each other becomes exponentially small, immeasurable. But it never goes away. On the microscopic scale, those interactions are still there, and they matter: quantum theory calls them interference.

拿抛硬币来说。硬币落地之后，看到正面的那个你和看到反面的那个你不再相互作用——它们对彼此的影响变得指数级微小，测不出来。但它永远不会消失。在微观尺度上，这些相互作用还在，而且很重要：量子理论称之为干涉（interference）。

SCENE 2 · THE STORYWhich one is the real me?哪一个才是真的我？

Kuhn asks the question anyone would ask. If there are many copies of me, which one is the real me? How can there be more than one "I," when I have a single, unitary consciousness?

库恩问了任何人都会问的问题：如果有很多个我，哪一个才是真的我？我只有一个统一的意识，怎么会有不止一个"我"？

Deutsch's answer is that philosophers have been asking the same question about time since time immemorial. Is the "I" of ten years ago — who did something embarrassing that I would never do today — really me? If a criminal repents, is it the same person, and should that person be punished? These are all questions about whether entities at different times are the same entity.

Deutsch 的回答是：哲学家们关于时间，早就问过同一个问题。十年前的那个"我"——做了件让我尴尬、今天绝不会做的事——真的是我吗？一个罪犯悔改了，还是同一个人吗，还该受罚吗？这些问题全都是：不同时间的实体，是不是同一个实体。

And here is the startling part. In the approximate theories that might be like quantum gravity, different times of the same universe appear in the theory in exactly the same way as different universes at the same time. There is no fundamental difference between them. Yesterday's universe is the same kind of object as an alternative universe today.

惊人的部分在这里。在那些可能像量子引力理论的近似理论里，同一个宇宙的不同时间，和同一时间的不同宇宙，在理论里以完全相同的方式出现。它们之间没有根本区别。昨天的宇宙和今天的另一个宇宙，是同一种东西。

So the flow of time is an emergent property of the multiverse. The multiverse is the name the theory gives to the whole of physical reality — a very complex object, not described by ordinary numbers like "three cats," but by mathematical entities that describe vast numbers of objects at the same time, in the same formalism, and their interactions with each other. We cannot think of the multiverse as being in time. Time is a feature of the multiverse.

所以时间流逝是多重宇宙的涌现（emergent）属性。多重宇宙是这个理论给整个物理实在起的名字——一个非常复杂的对象，不是用"三只猫"这样的普通数字描述的，而是用数学实体描述的：同一套形式同时描述无数个对象，以及它们之间的相互作用。我们不能把多重宇宙想成"在时间里"，时间是多重宇宙的一个特征。

SCENE 3 · THE TURNThere is no "ultimate reality" to find没有"终极实在"可找

Kuhn then asks the biggest question of all: what is ultimate reality? Deutsch refuses the question. He does not attempt to find ultimate reality, he says, because it is a kind of chimera. Start with problems. Start with what we know. Then try to make it more fundamental than that.

库恩接着问了最大的问题：终极实在是什么？Deutsch 拒绝了这个问题。他说，他并不试图寻找终极实在，因为那是一种幻兽（chimera）。从问题出发，从我们知道的东西出发，然后试着让它更根本。

What does "fundamental" mean, then? A fundamental idea is one that is needed in the explanation of many other ideas, many other phenomena. The most fundamental ones we know are the ones needed in the explanation of practically everything. And Deutsch picked out four: quantum physics, the theory of evolution, the theory of computation, and the theory of knowledge — four strands forming one unified fabric of reality, where none of them can be understood without the other three.

那"根本"是什么意思？一个根本的想法，是解释许多别的想法、许多别的现象时需要的想法。我们知道的最根本的想法，是解释几乎一切时都需要的想法。Deutsch 挑出了四个：量子物理学、进化论、计算理论和知识论——四根线织成一块统一的实在之布，其中任何一根都离不开另外三根，无法单独理解。

At first sight they look like different kinds of things. Three of the four seem tied to us humans, or to life on Earth, while the fourth is universal. But the closer you look, the more you realize you cannot understand any of them without the others. They are all intimately related — and each of them has been underestimated, accepted in its own field but not taken seriously as part of people's worldview. People accept quantum theory but not its parallel universes. People accept the universality of computation but not that it implies artificial intelligence is possible.

乍一看，它们像是不同种类的东西。四根线里有三根似乎和人类、和地球上的生命有关，只有一根是普遍的。但越仔细看，越发现不理解另外几根，就理解不了其中任何一根。它们紧密相连——而且每一根都被低估了：在各自的领域里被接受，却没有被认真当作世界观的一部分。人们接受量子理论，却不接受它的平行宇宙；人们接受计算的普遍性，却不接受它意味着人工智能是可能的。

SCENE 4 · THE CONTRASTIs the cosmos a computer?宇宙是一台计算机吗？

Computation, Deutsch says, is built into the laws of physics at a fundamental level. Many people hear that and jump to a conclusion: the reason mathematics works so well in physics is that the world is a computer, and we are just programs running in it — a simulation.

Deutsch 说，计算在最根本的层面上就内嵌于物理定律。很多人一听就跳到一个结论：数学在物理学里这么好用，是因为世界就是一台计算机，我们只是里面跑的程序——一场模拟。

That misses the whole point, he says. The simulation story needs a notion of what is or is not computable that sits outside the physical world — set by God, or something, to be a certain set, which is why our universe only instantiates those relationships. That does not solve the problem. You may as well have said that God set up just our universe with those relationships. Kuhn's reply: eliminate the middleman.

他说，那完全搞错了重点。模拟的说法需要一个"什么可算、什么不可算"的概念，放在物理世界之外——由上帝或什么设定好是某一套，所以我们的宇宙只实例化了那些关系。这没有解决问题，还不如直接说上帝就是这样设定了我们的宇宙。库恩接了一句：去掉中间商。

The real lesson is stranger and more exciting. A universal computer can be built within the universe. The set of all its possible motions — that is, the set of all possible programs that could be programmed into it — is in one-to-one correspondence with the set of all possible motions of anything. That is telling us something about the universe from the inside: something about what laws of nature actually are.

真正的教训更奇怪，也更激动人心。一台通用计算机（universal computer）可以在宇宙内部造出来。它所有可能的运动的集合——也就是能编进它的所有程序的集合——与任何东西所有可能的运动的集合一一对应。这是在从内部告诉我们关于宇宙的事：关于自然定律到底是什么。

And computation was not always seen as physics. When Babbage and then Turing built the theory in the 1930s, it was invented as a branch of mathematics, to study mathematical proofs. The mathematicians were applying their intuition about physical objects without knowing it — they thought about computing as making marks on squares of paper, and as Feynman remarked, they thought they understood paper. But paper, like everything else, obeys quantum mechanics. The theory of computation is the theory of quantum computation, and that is a theory of physics.

而计算并不一直被看作物理学。巴贝奇、然后是图灵在 1930 年代建立这个理论时，它是作为数学的一个分支被发明的，用来研究数学证明。数学家们在不知不觉中运用着他们对物理对象的直觉——他们把计算想成在纸格上做记号，用费曼的话说，他们以为自己懂纸。但纸和一切东西一样，都服从量子力学。计算理论就是量子计算理论，而那是物理学理论。

SCENE 5 · THE RISEA few thousand qubits beat the visible universe几千个量子比特胜过整个可见宇宙

So what does the quantum part add? For certain types of calculation, a quantum computer can work exponentially faster than any classical computer. A small quantum computer with a few thousand qubits — the quantum analogue of bits — could perform more computations simultaneously than could be performed by the entire visible universe, if it was all made into classical computers. And "more" is an understatement: exponentially more. But only for certain types of computation — and quantum computers are not slower for any computation, because a quantum computer can simulate a classical one.

那么量子那部分加了什么？对某些类型的计算，量子计算机可以比任何经典计算机指数级更快。一台只有几千个量子比特（qubit，比特的量子对应物）的小量子计算机，能同时做的计算，比整个可见宇宙全都变成经典计算机能做的还要多。而且"更多"还是说轻了：是指数级更多。但只对某些类型的计算——而且量子计算机对任何计算都不更慢，因为量子计算机可以模拟经典计算机。

That is why the quantum is so strange: it is not a weirder kind of ordinary physics. It changes what computation itself is. And computation, remember, is one of the four strands — which brings us to the fourth strand, knowledge. Of all the transformations the laws of physics permit — from hot to cold, from a block of marble into a statue — the overwhelming majority only happen if the right knowledge is present. Knowledge is information that can do things: adaptations in living things are a form of knowledge, so DNA embodies knowledge, human brains embody knowledge, books and computers and the internet embody knowledge.

这就是量子为什么这么奇怪：它不是一种更怪的普通物理学，它改变了计算本身是什么。而计算，记得吗，是四根线之一——这就把我们带到第四根线，知识。在物理定律允许的所有变化里——从热到冷、从一块大理石到一尊雕像——绝大多数只有在正确的知识在场时才会发生。知识是能做事的信息：生物体内的适应是一种知识，所以 DNA 承载着知识，人脑承载着知识，书、计算机、互联网都承载着知识。

That is why knowledge is a fundamental thing in the physical world — and why this book exists. The fourteen interviews in this volume are fourteen windows onto the same thing: knowledge, what it can do, and how far it can reach. The next night asks the question that follows naturally: if knowledge is fundamental, is there any limit to it at all? Night 2: Micah Redding — humanity's infinite reach.

这就是为什么知识是物理世界里一个根本的东西——也是这本书存在的原因。这卷书里的十四篇访谈，是看向同一件事的十四扇窗：知识、知识能做什么、知识能走多远。下一夜问的是自然而然接下来的问题：如果知识是根本的，它有没有尽头？第二夜：迈卡·雷丁——人类无限的 reach。

TAKEAWAY · 今夜带走三句

  1. Universes do not branch or split — they differentiate. The rapidly growing differentiation is the arrow of time, and the flow of time is an emergent property of the multiverse.宇宙不是分支、不是分裂，而是分化。分化程度的快速增长就是时间之箭，时间流逝是多重宇宙的涌现属性。
  2. "Ultimate reality" is a chimera. "Fundamental" means needed in the explanation of practically everything — and Deutsch's four strands (quantum physics, evolution, computation, knowledge) cannot be understood without each other."终极实在"是一种幻兽。"根本"的意思是解释几乎一切时都需要它——而 Deutsch 的四根线（量子物理学、进化论、计算理论、知识论）离不开彼此，无法单独理解。
  3. Knowledge is fundamental: most transformations the laws of physics allow only happen if the right knowledge is present. And a universal computer can be built inside the universe — the world is not a simulation running on one.知识是根本的：物理定律允许的大多数变化，只有在正确的知识在场时才会发生。而通用计算机可以在宇宙内部造出来——世界不是跑在一台计算机上的模拟。

HIGHLIGHTS · 十二句重点亮点

  1. Kuhn's opening difficulty: innumerable universes, continuously branching — very difficult to understand.库恩开场的难处：无数个宇宙、不断分支——非常难理解。
  2. They do not branch or split; they differentiate — at the beginning of time they may all have been identical.它们不是分支、不是分裂，而是分化——在时间之初，它们可能全都一模一样。
  3. The total number of distinct universes is finite, though enormous; the total number of universes is infinite — the multiverse keeps subdividing itself without limit.不同宇宙的总数是有限的，尽管大得惊人；宇宙的总数是无限的——多重宇宙不断细分，没有尽头。
  4. After a coin toss, the two versions stop interacting — the effect becomes exponentially small, but it never goes away; on the microscopic scale it is interference.抛硬币之后，两个版本不再相互作用——影响变得指数级微小，但永远不会消失；在微观尺度上，它就是干涉。
  5. "Which one is the real me?" is the same question philosophers asked about time: is the me of ten years ago really me?"哪一个才是真的我？"和哲学家关于时间问过的问题是同一个：十年前的那个我，真的是我吗？
  6. There is no fundamental difference between different times of the same universe and different universes at the same time.同一个宇宙的不同时间，和同一时间的不同宇宙之间，没有根本区别。
  7. The flow of time is an emergent property of the multiverse — time is a feature of the multiverse, not something the multiverse sits in.时间流逝是多重宇宙的涌现属性——时间是多重宇宙的一个特征，不是多重宇宙坐在里面的东西。
  8. Ultimate reality is a chimera — start with problems, start with what you know.终极实在是一种幻兽——从问题出发，从你知道的东西出发。
  9. The four strands of the fabric of reality: quantum physics, the theory of evolution, the theory of computation, and the theory of knowledge.实在之布的四根线：量子物理学、进化论、计算理论、知识论。
  10. Knowledge is information that can do things — DNA embodies it, brains embody it, books and computers embody it.知识是能做事的信息——DNA 承载它，大脑承载它，书和计算机承载它。
  11. The world is not a computer running a simulation — that needs computability set from outside physics; the real lesson is that a universal computer can be built inside the universe.世界不是跑在一台计算机上的模拟——那需要把可算性放在物理世界之外设定；真正的教训是通用计算机可以在宇宙内部造出来。
  12. A weak quantum computer with a few thousand qubits could do more computations at once than the entire visible universe made into classical computers.一台只有几千个量子比特的弱量子计算机，能同时做的计算比整个可见宇宙全变成经典计算机还要多。

01｜What does Deutsch mean when he says universes "differentiate" rather than "branch" — and what is the arrow of time in this picture?01｜Deutsch 说宇宙是"分化"而不是"分支"，是什么意思——在这个图像里，时间之箭是什么？
The universes may all have been identical at the beginning; physical processes made them different from each other. The total number stays constant, but the degree of differentiation grows very rapidly — and that growth is what we call the arrow of time. After a coin toss, the versions stop interacting (the effect becomes exponentially small, never zero); on the microscopic scale those interactions are interference.
宇宙在时间之初可能全都一模一样，物理过程让它们彼此不同。总数不变，但分化程度增长得非常快——这种增长就是我们说的时间之箭。抛硬币之后，两个版本不再相互作用（影响变得指数级微小，永远不为零）；在微观尺度上，这些相互作用就是干涉。
02｜Why does Deutsch call "ultimate reality" a chimera — and what does "fundamental" mean to him instead?02｜为什么 Deutsch 说"终极实在"是一种幻兽——"根本"在他那里是什么意思？
He does not attempt to find ultimate reality; start with problems and with what we know, then try to make it more fundamental. A fundamental idea is one needed in the explanation of many other ideas and phenomena — the most fundamental are needed to explain practically everything. His four: quantum physics, evolution, computation, and knowledge, none understandable without the other three.
他并不试图寻找终极实在；从问题出发，从我们知道的东西出发，然后试着让它更根本。一个根本的想法，是解释许多别的想法和现象时需要的想法——最根本的，是解释几乎一切时都需要的。他的四个：量子物理学、进化论、计算理论、知识论，任何一个都离不开另外三个。
03｜Why is "we are living in a simulation" the wrong lesson to draw from quantum computation — and what is the right one?03｜为什么"我们活在一场模拟里"是从量子计算得出的错误教训——正确的教训是什么？
The simulation story needs computability set from outside the physical world — by God or something — which solves nothing; you may as well say God set up the universe that way. The real lesson: a universal computer can be built within the universe, and all its possible programs correspond one-to-one with all possible motions of anything — telling us, from the inside, what laws of nature are.
模拟的说法需要把可算性放在物理世界之外设定——由上帝或什么设定——这什么都没解决，还不如直接说上帝就是这样设定了宇宙。真正的教训是：通用计算机可以在宇宙内部造出来，它所有可能的程序与任何东西所有可能的运动一一对应——从内部告诉我们，自然定律到底是什么。
