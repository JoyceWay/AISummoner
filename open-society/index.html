NIGHT 01 · 第一夜

CHAPTER 1 · HISTORICISM AND THE MYTH OF DESTINY历史主义与命运神话

Tonight answers just one question: does history have a destiny — a script already written? The answer starts in wartime exile, with a people who believed they were chosen.

今夜只回答一个问题：历史有命运吗——剧本是不是早就写好了？答案从战时流亡开始，从一个相信自己被选中的民族开始。

SCENE 1 · OPENINGA book written while the world burned战火中写成的书

In 1945, as the Second World War ended, a book appeared in London that had been written far away, in New Zealand. Its author, Karl Popper (1902–1994), was an Austrian who had fled the Nazis and found refuge on the other side of the world.

1945 年，第二次世界大战结束时，一本书在伦敦出版，它写于遥远的新西兰。作者卡尔·波普尔（1902–1994），一个逃离纳粹、在世界另一端找到庇护的奥地利人。

Popper called the book his contribution to the war effort — not a weapon, but an argument. While armies fought totalitarian regimes, he wanted to fight the idea underneath them: the belief that history moves by iron laws toward a destiny, and that those who know the laws may force history toward its end.

波普尔说这本书是他对战争的贡献——不是武器，而是一个论证。当军队在与极权政权作战时，他想打的是政权底下的那个想法：历史按铁律走向命运，懂得规律的人可以强行把历史推向终点。

The book's enemy has a name: historicism — the doctrine that history unfolds according to inexorable laws, and that its course can be predicted. Tonight is about that one word, and the myth that comes with it.

这本书的敌人有一个名字：historicism（历史主义）——认为历史按不可抗拒的规律展开、其进程可以预测的学说。今夜讲的就是这一个词，和随它而来的神话。

SCENE 2 · THE STORYThe chosen people被选中的民族

Popper begins with the oldest version of the idea: the doctrine of the chosen people. In this story, God is the author of history, and one people is His chosen instrument — picked out to carry out His plan.

波普尔从这个想法最古老的版本讲起：被选中的民族。在这个故事里，上帝是历史的作者，一个民族是祂选中的工具——被挑出来执行祂的计划。

Why would anyone believe that? Because of tribalism — the feeling that the individual means nothing alone, and everything only inside the tribe, the nation, the race, the class. When a group sees itself as chosen, its history stops looking like chance and starts looking like destiny.

为什么有人会信这个？因为tribalism（部落主义）——个人单独存在时毫无意义，只有在部落、民族、种族、阶级里面才有意义的感觉。当一个群体把自己看作被选中的，它的经历就不再像偶然，而像命运。

Then the story changes clothes. God steps out, and other forces step in: Nature, the Spirit, the laws of economics. The chosen people becomes the chosen race — and then, in Marx, the chosen class. The costumes change; the plot stays the same: history has a script, and one group holds the leading role.

然后故事换了衣服。上帝退场，别的力量登场：自然、精神、经济规律。被选中的民族变成被选中的种族——再到马克思那里，变成被选中的阶级。戏服换了，剧情没变：历史有剧本，有一个群体拿着主角的戏份。

That is the first thing to notice: historicism is not one theory. It is a shape that many theories take — a story in which the ending is already known.

这是首先要注意的：历史主义不是一个理论，而是一种许多理论都会套用的形状——一个结局早已写好的故事。

SCENE 3 · THE TURNA theory that can never fail永远不会错的理论

Here is the turn. Ask a historicist: what would prove you wrong? The honest answer is: nothing. If the revolution comes, the laws are confirmed. If it is delayed, the laws are merely working in hidden ways. If the opposite happens, it was a necessary detour on the road to destiny.

转折在这里。问一个历史主义者：什么能证明你错了？诚实的答案是：什么都不能。革命来了，规律得到证实；革命推迟了，规律只是在暗中起作用；发生了相反的事，那是通往命运之路上的必要弯路。

A theory that can accommodate any outcome explains nothing. It is a prophecy dressed up as science: it sounds like knowledge of the future, but no future event could ever contradict it — because every event is reinterpreted to fit.

一个能容纳任何结果的理论，什么都没解释。它是扮成科学的预言：听起来像对未来的知识，可没有任何未来事件能反驳它——因为每件事都会被重新解释，以符合理论。

And that is exactly why it is dangerous. If you believe you know where history must go, then forcing people toward that end starts to look justified — even necessary. False certainty about the future becomes a license for power in the present.

而这正是它危险的原因。如果你相信自己知道历史必然走向哪里，强迫人们走向那个终点就开始显得正当——甚至必要。对未来的虚假确定性，变成了当下权力的许可证。

SCENE 4 · THE CONTRASTThe closed tribe and the open society封闭的部落与开放的社会

Behind historicism stands an older picture of society: the closed society. It is tribal and magical. Its rules present themselves as natural and unchangeable, its customs are fixed, and criticism feels like betrayal — because to question the order is to question the tribe itself.

历史主义背后站着一幅更古老的社会图景：closed society（封闭社会）。它是部落的、魔法的。它的规则以自然、不可改变的面貌出现，习俗固定不变，批评感觉像背叛——因为质疑秩序就是质疑部落本身。

Against it Popper sets the open society: a society of criticism and choice. Its institutions are recognized as human artifacts — made by people, and therefore changeable. Its laws are revisable. Its members are free to question everything, including the foundations of the society itself.

波普尔用open society（开放社会）与之对立：一个批评与选择的社会。它的制度被认作人为的产物——人造的，所以可以改变。它的法律可以修改。它的成员可以自由地质疑一切，包括社会本身的基础。

The move from the closed society to the open one, Popper argues, is the great drama of Western history — and the great reactions against it, from Plato to Marx, are the subject of this whole book. The enemies of the open society are not just rulers. They are ideas — ideas that promise certainty and deliver closure.

波普尔认为，从封闭社会走向开放社会，是西方历史的大戏——而反对它的巨大反动，从柏拉图到马克思，就是整本书的主题。开放社会的敌人不只是统治者，而是一些想法——许诺确定性、带来封闭的想法。

SCENE 5 · THE RISESmall steps instead of a blueprint蓝图换成小步

If history has no script, how should we change society? Popper's answer is already hiding in this first chapter: not by utopian engineering — drawing up a blueprint of the perfect society and rebuilding everything at once — but by piecemeal engineering: small, reversible reforms, tested one at a time.

如果历史没有剧本，我们该怎么改变社会？波普尔的答案已经藏在第一章里：不是靠utopian engineering（乌托邦工程）——画一张完美社会的蓝图、一次性重建一切——而是靠piecemeal engineering（零星社会工程）：一小步、一小步、可逆的改革，一次检验一个。

The difference is practical. A blueprint for everything cannot be tested: when it goes wrong, there are too many moving parts to know why — and the failure is blamed on enemies, or on people who did not believe hard enough. A small reform can be tested: did it reduce the suffering it aimed at? If not, correct it, and try again.

区别是实际的。一张包罗万象的蓝图无法检验：它出错时，动的零件太多，找不出原因——失败只会被怪到敌人头上，或怪到信得不够坚定的人头上。而一个小改革可以检验：它减少了它要针对的苦难吗？没有，就纠正它，再试一次。

That is the book's promise, and its gamble: freedom does not live in knowing the future. It lives in keeping the future open — in societies that can admit a mistake, change course, and try again. The next night goes back to where historicism began: ancient Greece, and a philosopher who said everything flows.

这就是这本书的许诺，也是它的赌注：自由不在于知道未来，而在于让未来保持开放——在于一个能承认错误、改变方向、再试一次的社会。下一夜回到历史主义开始的地方：古希腊，和一位说万物皆流的哲学家。

TAKEAWAY · 今夜带走三句

  1. Historicism is the doctrine that history unfolds by inexorable laws — and that its course can be predicted. Popper's whole book is the argument that there are no such laws.历史主义是认为历史按不可抗拒的规律展开、其进程可以预测的学说。波普尔整本书就是论证：没有这样的规律。
  2. The myth of destiny wears many costumes — the chosen people, the chosen race, the chosen class. The costumes change; the plot stays the same.命运神话穿过许多戏服——被选中的民族、被选中的种族、被选中的阶级。戏服换了，剧情没变。
  3. A theory that can explain any outcome explains nothing. And a society that cannot be criticized cannot correct its mistakes — so keep it open, and change it one small step at a time.能解释任何结果的理论什么都没解释。而一个不能被批评的社会无法纠正错误——所以让它保持开放，一小步一小步地改变它。

01｜What is historicism, and what is the "myth of destiny"?01｜什么是历史主义，什么是“命运神话”？
Historicism is the doctrine that history unfolds according to inexorable laws and its course can be predicted. The myth of destiny is the story that comes with it: history has a script and a chosen carrier — a people, a race, a class — whose fate is to carry it out.
历史主义是认为历史按不可抗拒的规律展开、其进程可以预测的学说。命运神话是随它而来的故事：历史有剧本，有一个被选中的承担者——民族、种族、阶级——命中注定要执行它。
02｜Why are historicist theories dangerous, even though they sound like science?02｜历史主义理论听起来像科学，为什么还危险？
Because they are irrefutable: any outcome fits, so no event can prove them wrong — they only look like knowledge. And once you believe you know where history must go, forcing people toward that end starts to look justified.
因为它们无法反驳：任何结果都符合，所以没有任何事件能证明它们错——它们只是看起来像知识。而一旦你相信自己知道历史必然走向哪里，强迫人们走向那个终点就开始显得正当。
03｜What is the difference between a closed society and an open society — and between utopian and piecemeal engineering?03｜封闭社会和开放社会有什么区别——乌托邦工程和零星工程有什么区别？
In a closed society the order looks natural and unchangeable, and criticism feels like betrayal; in an open society institutions are human-made and revisable, and members may question everything. Utopian engineering rebuilds everything from a blueprint and cannot be tested; piecemeal engineering makes small, reversible reforms and corrects them by their results.
封闭社会里秩序看似自然、不可改变，批评像背叛；开放社会里制度是人造的、可以修改，成员可以质疑一切。乌托邦工程按蓝图重建一切，无法检验；零星工程做一小步、可逆的改革，按结果纠正。
