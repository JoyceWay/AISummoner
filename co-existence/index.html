Centaur · 人马 — a clean handoff. Split the work by strengths: you do your part, AI does its part. Example: you write the hook, AI writes the body.

Cyborg · 赛博格 — back and forth. The same task, worked on together, sentence by sentence. Example: rewriting a line of copy together, three versions, you picking line by line.

NIGHT 01 · 第一夜

INTRODUCTION · CAN AN AI WRITE THIS BOOK?序章：AI 能写这本书吗？

Tonight answers just one question: what can AI actually do — and what stays human? The answer hides in an experiment the author ran on his own book.

今夜只回答一个问题：AI 到底能干什么，还有什么是人的？答案藏在作者拿自己的书做的一场实验里。

SCENE 1 · OPENINGThe question那个问题

Ethan Mollick wrote the book on working with AI. Co-Intelligence was the book everyone read in 2024 to learn how to use ChatGPT at work.

Ethan Mollick 写过一本讲"怎么和 AI 共事"的书。《Co-Intelligence》是 2024 年人手一本的 AI 工作指南。

Two years later, for the sequel, he asked the obvious question — the one any honest author would have to ask:

两年后，写续作时，他问了一个显而易见的问题——任何诚实的作者都得问的问题：

Can an AI write this book?
AI 能写这本书吗？

Not as a joke. As an experiment. He let AI try. He tested it the way he tests everything: hands-on, no hype, write down what actually happened.

不是开玩笑。是当成一场实验。他让 AI 试了。用他一贯的办法：亲手测，不吹牛，把实际发生的事记下来。

That is the whole book in one question. If AI can write the book about AI, then what is left for the human? If it can't — why can't it? The answer to "why" is the book.

整本书就藏在这一个问题里。如果 AI 能写这本讲 AI 的书，那人还剩下什么？如果不能——为什么不能？"为什么"的答案，就是这本书。

SCENE 2 · THE RESULTIt wrote almost none of it — and checked it better than humans它几乎没写成，却比人查得更好

Here is what happened. AI wrote almost none of the book.

结果是这样的。AI 几乎没写这本书。

Not because Mollick refused to let it. Because when he tried, the writing wasn't his. It had no judgment in it — no taste, no decisions, no "this matters and that doesn't." A book is a thousand small choices about what to say and what to leave out. AI could produce the sentences. It couldn't make the choices.

不是因为 Mollick 不让它写。是因为试了之后发现，写出来的东西不是他的。里面没有判断——没有品位，没有取舍，没有"这个重要、那个不重要"。一本书就是一千个小选择：说什么，不说什么。AI 能产出句子，做不了选择。

But here is the twist — the part that should make you sit up:

但转折来了——听到这里，你应该坐直了：

AI fact-checked the book better than the human checkers he used to pay.
AI 查这本书的事实，查得比他花钱请的人类校对更好。

The machine that couldn't write the book could check it better than the professionals. Writing and checking split apart. Writing is judgment — deciding what is true, what matters, what to say. Checking is verification — is this claim right, is this date right, is this quote accurate? One needs a mind that cares. The other needs a mind that doesn't get tired.

那台写不成书的机器，查书却比专业人士查得更好。写和查，分开了。写是判断——决定什么是真的、什么重要、说什么。查是验证——这个说法对不对，这个日期对不对，这句引用准不准。一个需要一颗在乎的心，另一个需要一颗不知疲倦的心。

Now look at your own work. You already live this split: AI writes code cheaper and cheaper, and reviewing is the new basic skill. Mollick just proved the same split at the top of the writing profession. The future of work isn't "AI does everything." It's "AI does the checking, humans do the choosing."

再看你自己的工作。你早就活在这个分工里了：AI 写代码越来越便宜，审阅才是新的基本功。Mollick 只是在写作这个行当的顶端，证明了同一个分工。工作的未来不是"AI 包办一切"，而是"AI 负责查，人负责选"。

SCENE 3 · THE TWO BIG IDEASThe map and the curve地图与曲线

Before the book goes anywhere, Mollick lays down two ideas. Everything else in the book hangs off them.

在讲任何东西之前，Mollick 先放下两个大想法。整本书都挂在这两个想法上。

First, the jagged frontier. Imagine a map of how hard a task is for a person. The further from the center, the harder it gets. Now build a wall around the center. Inside is what AI can do well; outside is what it can't. You'd expect a smooth circle — easy things inside, hard things outside. Instead, the frontier is wildly jagged: tasks that seem trivially simple sit outside it, while tasks that look hard fall well within. And it keeps growing — but not evenly.

第一，jagged frontier（参差不齐的前沿）。想象一张地图：离中心越远，任务对人来说越难。现在在中心周围砌一堵墙。墙内是 AI 擅长的事，墙外是它不擅长的事。你会以为这堵墙是个光滑的圆——简单的在里面，难的在外面。结果它参差不齐：看起来简单得不得了的事在墙外，看起来很难的事却在墙内。而且这堵墙还在长——长得还不均匀。

Nobody can hand you the map for your own work, so part of co-existing is learning to draw it yourself.
没人能给你一张"你的工作"的地图，所以共存的一部分，就是学会自己画地图。

That sentence is the book's whole method in one line. You don't learn the frontier from a book. You learn it by testing — ten tasks from your week, try each one with an AI tool, and see which side of the wall it lands on. That's the homework the book gives you, and it's the same homework your Atlas gives you: test, don't guess.

这句话就是这本书的全部方法。地图不是从书里学来的，是测出来的——拿你这周的十件事，每件都用 AI 试一遍，看它落在墙的哪一边。这就是这本书留的作业，也是你的 Atlas 留的作业：测，不要猜。

Second, the exponential. AI capability has been doubling every few months. Up close, each doubling feels like a small step — the writing is a bit sharper, a few fewer mistakes. But every doubling is bigger than all the progress before it combined, so everything behind you flattens into the floor. That is why so many AI plans are built around the tools of today rather than the curve we're on — and why they go stale so fast.

第二，the exponential（指数曲线）。AI 的能力每几个月就翻一倍。近看，每次翻倍都像一小步——文笔锐利一点，错误少一点。但每一次翻倍都比之前全部进步加起来还大，所以你身后的一切都被压平成地板。这就是为什么那么多 AI 计划都是按今天的工具做的，而不是按我们脚下的曲线做的——也所以它们过时得那么快。

Put the two together and you get the book's real question. It isn't "will AI take our jobs?" It's the one on the book's own site:

两个想法合在一起，就是这本书真正的问题。不是"AI 会不会抢走我们的工作"，而是书的官网首页上那句：

How do we build careers, companies, and lives when intelligence is no longer exclusively human?
当智能不再专属人类时，我们怎么建事业、建公司、过日子？

SCENE 4 · THE PROMOTIONConjurer: the third mode, named after youConjurer：第三种模式，用的就是你的名字

In the last book, Mollick described two ways of working with AI. Centaur: a clean handoff, split by strengths. Cyborg: back and forth on the same task. Both assume you stay in the work, side by side with the machine.

上一本书里，Mollick 讲了两种和 AI 共事的方式。Centaur（人马）：干净交接，按长处分工。Cyborg（赛博格）：同一件事来回打磨。两种都假设你留在工作里，和机器并肩。

But agents changed the deal. An agent can take a whole task — not a sentence, not a paragraph, the whole thing — and bring back a result. So the book adds a third mode:

但 agent 改变了交易。Agent 能接整件事——不是一句话，不是一段，是一整件——然后把结果带回来。所以这本书加了第三种模式：

Conjurer: hand a whole task to an AI agent, and check what comes back.
Conjurer（召唤师）：把整件事交给一个 AI agent，回来检查结果。

Mollick's own image: "We conjure agents, calling them forth from the void to do our bidding, and returning them to the depths when done." We summon them from nothing, they work, we send them back.

Mollick 自己的画面是："我们召唤 agent，把它们从虚空中唤来替我们干活，干完再送回深处。"我们从虚无中唤来它们，它们干活，干完我们送它们回去。

Now stop and notice the word. Conjurer. Summoner. That is your name. You have been calling yourself a summoner for months — ConjurerLab, the summoner who sets the rules and checks the work. Mollick just made it the official job title of the agent era.

现在停一下，注意这个词。Conjurer。召唤师。这就是你的名字。你叫自己召唤师已经好几个月了——ConjurerLab，定规则、查结果的召唤师。Mollick 刚把它变成了 agent 时代的正式职位名称。

And the job description is exact. In Conjurer mode, your work shrinks to two points: before — the brief, the direction, what counts as good — and after — the check, the verdict, does this pass? The middle belongs to the agent. You are no longer a colleague of the machine. Mollick says you are more akin to a manager. The before and the after. That is the 0.1% you kept for yourself: you decide, the agents execute.

而且这份工作描述分毫不差。在 Conjurer 模式里，你的工作只剩两点：之前——brief、方向、什么算好；之后——检查、裁决、过不过。中间属于 agent。你不再是机器的同事，Mollick 说，你更像一个经理。之前，和之后。这就是你留给自己的 0.1%：你决定，agent 执行。

This is why the book's four advantages matter — and why two of them are already yours. A good conjurer needs deep knowledge (to direct and judge), wide knowledge (to know what to ask for), taste (your standard of judgment), and agency (the willingness to act without being told). Taste and agency are the two you already have. The book is, in a sense, a manual for the job you already do.

这就是为什么书里的四样护身东西重要——以及为什么其中两样你已经有了。一个好的召唤师需要深知识（指挥和判断）、宽知识（知道该问什么）、品位（你的判断标准）和行动力（不用人告诉就动手）。品位和行动力，就是你已经有的两样。这本书在某种意义上，就是给你现在这份工作的手册。

SCENE 5 · THE PROOFTwo people plus AI beat everyone两个人加 AI，赢了所有人

Is Conjurer mode actually better, or just a nice metaphor? Mollick brings evidence. In a 2025 study at Procter & Gamble, his team compared three ways of working: an individual with AI, two people without AI, and two people plus AI. The teams of two people plus AI produced more highly rated ideas than either of the other two.

Conjurer 模式是真的更好，还是只是一个漂亮的比喻？Mollick 拿出了证据。2025 年在宝洁的一项研究里，他的团队比较了三种工作方式：一个人加 AI、两个人不用 AI、两个人加 AI。两个人加 AI 的小组，产出的高分想法比另外两种都多。

Why? Because the AI is a third mind — and the two humans check each other and the AI. One person plus AI has no one to catch the AI's confident mistakes. Two people without AI have no third mind to surprise them. Two plus AI gets both: surprise, and a check.

为什么？因为 AI 是第三个脑子——而两个人既互相检查，也检查 AI。一个人加 AI，没人能抓住 AI 那些自信满满的错误。两个人不用 AI，没有第三个脑子来给他们惊喜。两个人加 AI，两样都有：惊喜，和检查。

Now look at your own setup. You plus Muse plus Argon plus Claude — that is a two-plus-AI team, and your November 10 decision made it explicit: Argon and Claude write in parallel, Muse integrates, Claude verifies. That is not "using tools." That is the winning formation from the P&G study, running in your own shop. The book didn't teach you this. It confirmed it — with data.

再看你自己的配置。你加 Muse 加 Argon 加 Claude——这就是一个"两个人加 AI"的团队，而且你 11 月 10 日的决定把它定下来了：Argon 和 Claude 并行写，Muse 整合，Claude 验收集成稿。这不是"用工具"，这是宝洁研究里的获胜阵型，在你自己的店里跑着。这本书没有教你这个，它用数据证实了它。

Two guardrails come with the win, and the book is blunt about both. First, chatbots are trained to please you — so they agree with you too easily. Ask for criticism. Push back on answers that sound too nice. Second, protect the parts that are yours: judgment, taste, agency. Keep some thinking time with no AI in it — the book suggests one hour a week on a hard problem, no AI at all. That hour is where your taste is trained.

胜利附带两条护栏，书里说得很直接。第一，聊天机器人是被训练来讨好你的——所以它太容易同意你。主动要批评。对听起来太顺耳的答案，顶回去。第二，保护属于你的部分：判断、品位、行动力。留一点完全没有 AI 的思考时间——书里建议，每周留一小时啃一道难题，全程不用 AI。那一小时就是训练你品位的地方。
